GymApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

GymApp is a strength training tracker for Android, Wear OS, and Garmin Connect IQ devices. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

GymApp may store workout information that you enter or generate while using the app, including:

  • workout dates, exercises, sets, reps, and weights;
  • training notes, progress statistics, missions, ranks, and achievements;
  • Garmin workout summaries such as duration, heart rate, calories, heart-rate zones, and set count;
  • sync status and diagnostic information needed to transfer workouts between your phone and watch.

Account and Cloud Sync

If you use cloud sync or leaderboard features, GymApp may use account and workout-related data to sync your training history between devices and show app features such as rankings or progress. Cloud features are used only to provide the app functionality you choose to use.

Garmin and Companion App Sync

The Garmin Connect IQ app can send completed workout summaries to the Android companion app. This may include workout duration, estimated GymApp calories, Garmin calories, average and maximum heart rate, heart-rate zones, exercise names, weight, reps, and set count.

How We Use Information

We use app data to:

  • save and display your workout history;
  • calculate progress, volume, personal records, recommendations, missions, and achievements;
  • sync workouts between supported Android, Wear OS, Garmin, and cloud components;
  • diagnose sync or app issues when you choose to share diagnostic information.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with services needed to operate app features, such as cloud sync infrastructure, or when required by law. Garmin data is handled through Garmin Connect IQ and the companion app communication flow.

Data Storage and Security

Workout data may be stored locally on your device and, if cloud sync is enabled, in the app's cloud backend. We use reasonable technical measures to protect stored and transmitted data, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You can stop using cloud sync, delete local app data, uninstall the app, or contact us to request help with account-related data. Some Garmin data can also be managed through Garmin's own apps and services.

Children

GymApp is not designed for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy when app features or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will reflect the latest version.
