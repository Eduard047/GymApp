## Description

Build a training routine you can understand, repeat, and improve.

GymApp brings workout planning, logging, progress, and motivation into one focused place. Record exercises, sets, weight, repetitions, and notes, then use your history to see what is improving and what to train next.

WORKOUT LOGGING
• Build sessions with multiple exercises and sets
• Use Push, Pull, Legs, Upper, Lower, and Deload templates
• Repeat your latest workout or copy a previous session
• Track weight, repetitions, volume, personal records, and rest timers

SMART TRAINING
• Create a local Training Profile for your split, weekly frequency, goal, and energy balance
• Get Smart Coach workout and set recommendations from your training history
• Review muscle load and manually refine exercise-to-muscle mappings

PROGRESS THAT STAYS USEFUL
• Explore monthly summaries, activity heatmaps, muscle breakdowns, and exercise charts
• Review workout details and post-workout summaries
• Export or import a JSON backup and share a readable diagnostics PDF

MOTIVATION
• Complete daily, weekly, and monthly missions
• Earn XP, levels, titles, achievements, and badges
• Compare public display names, XP, levels, and workout counts on the GymApp leaderboard

CLOUD AND GARMIN
• Sign in with email to sync your GymApp state securely through Supabase
• Optionally send workout plans to the existing paired Garmin cloud queue

GymApp contains no advertising, tracking, or in-app purchases. Rest-timer alerts use local notifications only and remain optional.

GymApp is a fitness log and planning tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any condition. Exercise within your abilities and consult a qualified professional before making health decisions.
